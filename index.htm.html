Budget Adjustments

An adjustment transfers budget between two specific WFP line items (Activity → Object of Expenditure → Description) for a specific quarter. The amount is deducted from the Source item's quarterly budget and added to the Recipient item's quarterly budget — it is not simply added on top of the total. All dashboard figures, the WFP Master List, release validation, and reports reflect these adjusted quarterly budgets.
